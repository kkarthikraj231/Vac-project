DEMO GUIDE
Before demonstration:
1. Open the published chatbot or Botpress test panel.
2. Keep the FAQ knowledge source visible in another tab.
3. Prepare screenshots of the chatbot builder and published chat.
4. Do not claim live order/payment integration unless you built and tested it.

Suggested 3-minute demonstration:
1. Introduce the problem and SmartShop demo.
2. Ask: “How long does delivery take?” Show a knowledge-grounded answer.
3. Ask: “How do I reset my password?” Show another FAQ answer.
4. Ask: “What is my exact order status?” Confirm the bot does not invent live data.
5. Ask: “Can I speak to a human?” Show fallback/support direction.
6. Explain how updating the FAQ updates the chatbot’s knowledge.

VIVA QUESTIONS AND SHORT ANSWERS
1. What is a knowledge base?
   A structured collection of verified information used to answer questions.
2. What is a no-code platform?
   A tool that lets users build applications through visual interfaces rather than writing code.
3. What is NLP?
   Natural Language Processing helps computers interpret human language.
4. What is generative AI?
   AI that creates new content, such as conversational answers.
5. What is RAG?
   Retrieval-Augmented Generation retrieves relevant information and uses it to ground a generated answer.
6. Why does the chatbot need a fallback?
   To avoid guessing when reliable information is unavailable.
7. What is the main limitation?
   It may misunderstand questions or lack updated information; complex cases need human support.
8. How do you evaluate it?
   Test known and unknown queries and measure accuracy, relevance, response time, and fallback behavior.
9. Is the sample company real?
   No. SmartShop is fictional and used for demonstration.
10. What is future scope?
    Multilingual support, secure integrations, analytics, and human-agent ticketing.
